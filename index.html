Why sharks win

Most bettors lose to the vig. Sharks beat the closing line.

At standard −110 pricing you need to win 52.38% of bets just to break even. Shopping five books typically saves 2–4% in price per bet, and over 500 bets that difference decides whether you win or lose. Shark.Bet is built around the three habits pros share:

  • Shop every line: take the best number, never the first one.
  • Bet only +EV: compare your price to no-vig fair odds.
  • Size with discipline: 1–2% units or fractional Kelly.
  • Track CLV: beating the close is the skill signal that holds up over time.

No. We never accept wagers. Shark.Bet is a free information, education and entertainment platform with calculators, odds intelligence and free-to-play contests.

What does "bet like a shark" mean?

"Shark" or "sharp" is betting slang for a disciplined, math-driven bettor. Sharks shop lines, bet only when they have an edge, size bets by bankroll, and measure themselves by closing line value.
