Core strategy

The five pillars of sharp betting

1. Price is everything

A bet is only good or bad relative to its price. Convert every line into implied probability, and only bet when your estimate is higher. At −110 you need 52.38% to break even; at −105 you need only 51.22%.

2. Line shopping is free money

Holding accounts at 4–6 books and always taking the best number is the easiest edge in betting. Moving from −110 to −105 on every bet adds roughly 2% ROI with no extra handicapping.

3. Closing line value (CLV)

The closing line is the most efficient price in the market. If you consistently get better odds than the close, you are very likely a long-term winner, even through losing streaks.

4. Bankroll discipline

Bet 1–2% of your bankroll per unit, or use fractional Kelly. A 10-bet losing streak happens to everyone, and proper sizing makes sure it doesn't end your season.

5. Track everything

Log odds taken, closing odds, stake and result. Track ROI and CLV by sport and bet type, then cut what loses and scale what wins.
