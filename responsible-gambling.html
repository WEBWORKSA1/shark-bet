Responsible Gambling

Real sharks know when not to bet. Gambling should be entertainment, never a way to make money you need or to escape problems.

Warning signs

  • Betting more than you planned, or chasing losses
  • Borrowing money or using bill money to bet
  • Hiding betting from family or friends
  • Feeling anxious, irritable or low when not betting
  • Betting to escape stress or problems

Shark rules for staying in control

  • Set a bankroll you can afford to lose entirely, and never top it up mid-month.
  • Use deposit, loss and time limits in every sportsbook account.
  • Keep units at 1–2% of bankroll.
  • Never bet under the influence or while upset.
  • Take regular breaks and track every bet.

Get help: free and confidential

  • USA: 1-800-GAMBLER (call/text) · ncpgambling.org
  • Canada (Ontario): ConnexOntario 1-866-531-2600 · connexontario.ca
  • UK: National Gambling Helpline 0808 8020 133 · BeGambleAware · self-exclusion via GAMSTOP
  • Australia: Gambling Help Online 1800 858 858 · BetStop
  • Worldwide: Gamblers Anonymous

Blocking tools

Software such as Gamban or BetBlocker can block gambling sites and apps on your devices. Most licensed sportsbooks also offer cool-off periods and self-exclusion in account settings.
