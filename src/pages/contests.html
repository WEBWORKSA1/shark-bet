Official contest rules (summary)

  • No purchase necessary. A purchase or payment does not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  • Eligibility: open to individuals who are 21+ (or the age of majority where they live) and live where free sweepstakes/skill contests are permitted. One entry per person per contest.
  • Scoring: Pick'em awards 1 point per correct pick against the posted spread; pushes score 0.5. The tiebreaker is the closest total without going over, then the earliest entry.
  • Prizes: as listed on each contest. Prizes are non-transferable; winners may need to confirm eligibility and are responsible for applicable taxes.
  • Winner notification: by email within 7 days of contest close. Unclaimed prizes after 14 days may be forfeited.
  • Conduct: multiple accounts, automation or collusion lead to disqualification.
  • Shark.Bet does not accept wagers. Contests are free-to-play entertainment. See the Terms for full terms.
