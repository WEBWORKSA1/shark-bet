How to read it

Three signals sharps watch

  • Best price. The same bet can differ by 10–20 cents across books. Always take the teal number.
  • Tickets vs money. Public bettors make many small bets; sharps make fewer, bigger ones. When money % is well above ticket %, big bettors are on that side.
  • Reverse line movement. If the line moves against the side most tickets are on, books are respecting sharp money.
