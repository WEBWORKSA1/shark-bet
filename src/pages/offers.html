Value a bonus like a shark

A "$1,000 bonus" is rarely worth $1,000

  • Bonus bets (stake not returned) convert to cash at roughly 70–80% of face value when you bet them on +300 to +500 odds.
  • "Second chance" / no-sweat offers are worth about 40–60% of the max refund.
  • Deposit matches with 10× playthrough are often worth less than 10% of face value.
  • Always read: minimum odds, expiry, and whether bonus-bet stakes are returned.
