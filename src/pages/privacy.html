Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information we collect

We collect information you give us through forms (name, email, region, preferences, contest picks, applications and messages) and basic technical data (browser type, pages visited) via cookies and analytics.

How we use it

  • To send the Starter Kit, Shark Report and contest communications you request
  • To run contests and contact winners
  • To reply to inquiries, applications and partnership requests
  • To improve the site and measure performance
  • Where you opt in, to send offers from licensed partners available in your region

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-forwarding service (FormSubmit). Donations are processed by PayPal under PayPal's privacy policy. We never see or store your card details.

Cookies & advertising

We use cookies for preferences (theme, age confirmation) and may use Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe from any email in one click, and request access to, correction of or deletion of your data through our contact page. EU/UK (GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA) and Canadian (PIPEDA) residents have additional rights, which we honour.

Age restriction

Shark.Bet is intended for adults 21+ (18+/19+ where applicable). We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Contact

Privacy questions: use our contact form.
