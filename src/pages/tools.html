Betting calculator FAQ

How do I convert American odds to decimal?

For positive odds, divide by 100 and add 1 (+150 = 2.50). For negative odds, divide 100 by the absolute value and add 1 (−110 = 1.909).

What is a hedge bet?

A hedge is a bet on the opposite outcome of an existing wager, sized to lock in profit or reduce risk whatever the result.

What is the Kelly criterion?

A staking formula: f = (bp − q) / b, where b is decimal odds minus 1, p is your win probability and q = 1 − p. Most sharps bet ¼ to ½ Kelly.

What are no-vig fair odds?

No-vig odds strip the bookmaker's margin out of a market by normalising the implied probabilities so they sum to 100%.

Why is my parlay payout lower than I expected?

Each leg carries the book's margin, and the margins multiply. A 4-leg parlay at −110 per leg carries roughly 17% hold versus about 4.5% on a single bet.
